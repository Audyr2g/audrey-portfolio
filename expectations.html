Question (A)

Deep Learning-Based Predictive Models

Response:

Deep learning predictive models have the ability to assist companies with one of the most difficult problems in business is losing customers. Customer churn occurs when current customers decide to cease making purchases at a particular organization and opt to make those same purchases elsewhere (i.e. purchase from a competitor). As this is the case for many industry types (telecommunications, banking, e-commerce and subscription based) it is usually less expensive for organizations to retain existing customers then acquire new customers. When an organization does not know which customers will be leaving that organization's customer base; the organization typically experiences decreased revenue, increased dissatisfaction among its customer base and ultimately lower profitability over time.

Deep Learning allows for a detailed review of a vast amount of historical Customer Data that includes customer purchase history, Browsing Behavior, customer feedback, and service usage. Patterns so that companies will know about Trends or Patterns that would have gone unnoticed with a Manual Review. With the ability to predict which Customers are most likely to leave a Company, Businesses are able to proactively offer Personalized Promotions to keep them from switching companies, offer improved customer support to solve any problems they are experiencing, and/or Reward Loyal Customers prior to deciding to switch to a competitor's company. While Deep Learning Models are often referred to as "Black Boxes" due to the fact that it is extremely difficult to understand how a Model makes its Decisions regarding a Prediction, Organizations can improve Transparency by applying Explainable AI (XAI) Techniques to Identify the Factors that contributed to each Prediction. Regular evaluation of models, supervision by humans, and ongoing tracking of predictions will also help ensure that the predictions remain accurate, fair and trustworthy. Enable organizations to make better business decisions while maintaining customer trust in AI-powered systems.

Question (B)

Natural Language Processing

Response:

Modern companies are developing smart, conversational artificial intelligence (AI), using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP). These conversational AIs have the ability to; identify a users' intent for a given interaction, remember previous conversations with the user and give customized answers in real-time. Conversational AI, powered by NLP is far beyond just using scripted answers like most traditional chatbots. The use of NLP means that an organization's conversational AI will be able to recognize various types of communication patterns, as well as comprehend the context of a conversation. As such, a conversational AI has the potential to customize its response to meet the unique needs of each user.

Natural Language Processing (NLP), in combination with Machine Learning (ML)/Deep Learning (DL) has also enabled business organizations to better understand customer perceptions about a company's offerings by analyzing customer comments received via e-mail and/or social media. Using NLP, as well as ML/DL, organizations can use past conversation patterns and new data collected from the user input process, to continuously refine the NLP model(s) for increased accuracy in assessing the intent behind a particular message. According to the authors of the required reading material, NLP has provided organizations the ability to analyze large volumes of un-structured text that previously could not be analyzed; which enables both humans and artificial intelligence systems to have more natural, context-based, and richly textured interactions.
